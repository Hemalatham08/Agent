# Product and SKU

## Product

A product is the commercial item a business buys, stores, or sells. It carries
the name, category, tax classification, and unit of measure used in
documentation and reporting.

## SKU

A SKU (stock keeping unit) is the identifier used to track a specific stocked
form of a product. One product may have several SKUs when it is stocked in
different pack sizes, colours, or warehouse configurations.

## Product versus SKU

The product answers "what is this?" and the SKU answers "which stocked form of
it moved?". Inventory movements, receipts, and dispatches are recorded against
SKUs so that counts remain unambiguous.

## Inactive records

Retiring a product or SKU marks it inactive instead of deleting it. Historical
documents continue to reference the retired record so that past transactions
stay readable.
